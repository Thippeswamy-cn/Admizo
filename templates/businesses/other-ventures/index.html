New ventures begin with an opportunity that needs to be examined carefully. A promising idea still needs a clear audience, a workable operating model and a realistic understanding of the resources it requires.

This division provides a starting point for enquiries about emerging initiatives and selected opportunities outside the other business areas. An enquiry opens a discussion; it does not imply an investment commitment or an agreement to participate.

{% endblock %} {% block business_details %}
01

Emerging initiative exploration

Early discussions can establish what the idea involves and why it may be useful. The initial scope should focus on the problem, intended audience and evidence already available.

  • Problem and proposed solution
  • Intended users or customers
  • Current stage and supporting evidence
02

Feasibility questions

Feasibility depends on demand, delivery requirements and practical constraints. Breaking an idea into testable questions helps identify where further investigation is needed.

  • Demand assumptions and validation needs
  • Operational and technical dependencies
  • Resource requirements and constraints
03

Collaboration opportunities

Potential collaborators need to understand the purpose of an initiative and the role being proposed. Clear responsibilities help establish whether there is a basis for further discussion.

  • Proposed contribution of each party
  • Capabilities and resources sought
  • Ownership and decision-making questions
04

Pilot and next-step planning

A limited pilot may help investigate a specific assumption before a larger commitment. Its scope should identify what will be evaluated and how a decision will be reached.

  • Pilot objective and boundaries
  • Review criteria and information requirements
  • Decision points and possible next steps
{% endblock %} {% block process %}

  • Introduce the idea

    Explain the problem, proposed approach and current stage in a concise brief.

  • Clarify the opportunity

    Discuss the audience, available evidence and what you are seeking from a collaboration.

  • Identify open questions

    List the feasibility, resource and responsibility questions that need resolution.

  • Choose a next step

    Determine whether further discussion, additional information or a defined evaluation is appropriate.

    {% endblock %} {% block questions %}
    Does ADMIEZO provide funding for every proposal?

    No. Submitting an enquiry does not imply funding, investment, endorsement or acceptance of a proposal.

    Can I submit an idea outside the listed divisions?

    Yes. Explain the opportunity and why you believe it is relevant. The team will need to assess fit and availability before any engagement.

    What information should I share first?

    Begin with a concise, non-confidential description of the problem, solution, current stage and proposed collaboration. Avoid sending proprietary technical details in an initial enquiry.
