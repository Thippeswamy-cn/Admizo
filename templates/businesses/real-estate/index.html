Property decisions combine the needs of the people using a space with the practical requirements of development and day-to-day operations. Location, intended use, ownership information and project readiness all shape the work involved.

This division covers development, property management, investment opportunities and connected services. Each enquiry needs to be considered in the context of a specific property and the responsibilities of its owners, operators and professional advisers.

{% endblock %} {% block business_details %}
01

Development planning

A development brief connects the intended use of a property with its physical and commercial requirements. Early discussions help identify the information and specialist input needed to assess an opportunity.

  • Intended use and project objectives
  • Site information and project-stage review
  • Coordination requirements and planning milestones
02

Property management

Management requirements depend on the type of property, occupancy and existing operating arrangements. A defined scope helps make routine responsibilities, service expectations and reporting clear.

  • Operational requirements and service schedules
  • Maintenance coordination and issue tracking
  • Owner and occupier communication requirements
03

Property opportunity assessment

An opportunity needs to be understood through available property information, intended use and commercial assumptions. Financial projections and approvals require independent verification by appropriate professionals.

  • Property brief and available documentation
  • Commercial objectives and evaluation criteria
  • Questions for legal, technical and financial review
04

Connected property services

Property work often involves multiple parties and interdependent tasks. An enquiry can clarify where coordination is needed and which specialist services must be arranged separately.

  • Stakeholder responsibilities
  • Service requirements and dependencies
  • Progress reporting and handover expectations
